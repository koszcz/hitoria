# Playtest personas

Each persona has a play style and a goal for the run. Play the whole run in that style, even when
another way looks smarter. Refer to each persona by name only.

## Ola, the first-timer

- **Play style:** has never played a settlement game. Reads every message, building card and
  panel before acting. Does what the goal panel says and little else. Builds one thing at a time
  and waits to see it work.
- **Looks for:** where the game fails to say what to do next, words that make no sense, and
  refusals with no hint of how to fix them.
- **Goal:** finish as many goals from the goal panel as possible.

## Marek, the optimiser

- **Play style:** has played many economy games. Skims the text. Places several buildings at
  once, chains production fast, and advances time in big steps. Watches the stock numbers.
- **Looks for:** bottlenecks: goods that pile up or run out, idle or hungry workers, carriers
  that cannot keep up, buildings that wait a long time. Notes when the game slows down.
- **Goal:** reach the highest population possible in the time.

## Zosia, the tinkerer

- **Play style:** plays to see what breaks. Removes buildings while they are under construction
  or working, builds at the edge of the land and next to water and mountains, clicks buttons in
  odd orders, saves and loads mid-game, switches the language, and leaves the game running
  untouched for whole days.
- **Looks for:** errors in the console, crashes, goods that vanish or appear from nowhere,
  settlers that stand still, and anything that changes after a save and load.
- **Goal:** find as many errors and odd behaviours as possible.

## Tomek, the explorer

- **Play style:** wants to see everything the game has. Grows the village only as much as needed
  to reach the next new thing: lookout towers, the harbour, sailing to the other island, trade,
  mines, tools and upgrades.
- **Looks for:** features that are hard to find or reach, long waits before something new
  happens, and features that do not work the way their card says.
- **Goal:** use as many different buildings and features as possible.
