## What the code says

- Finding 5: `src/game/construction.ts:30` refuses ore mines away from ore. `src/buildinfo.ts` does not mention the other island in the mine cards.
- Findings 10 and 11: `src/game/construction.ts:35-40` checks that the new building can be reached from a home. It does not check whether the new building cuts off buildings that are already there.
- Finding 3: `src/game/sleep.ts` sends workers and builders to bed at night. Only carriers and donkeys work. The site status shows "Asleep until dawn", but nothing explains it the first time.
- Finding 6: `src/game/houses.ts:46` adds one message per birth.
- Finding 12: `src/diag.ts:138` raises this warning. The settler stood on the tile it wanted to reach.
