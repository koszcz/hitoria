# Playtest: Zosia on island 250614263

- Commit: 7f53284 Start building Fish that run out
- Played: 10 minutes, to game time 2911 s (day 7) on island 250614263, then 65 s on island 958901770 and a third fresh island
- Goal for this run: find as many errors and odd behaviours as possible.

## What the tester achieved

1. "Make 50 planks" goal reached at 1591 s (day 4).
2. Settled the second island at 1481 s, by sailing from the Harbour.
3. Got the second island going again at about 2911 s, with a Fisher's Hut and a Stone Mine.
4. Started a new island with the "Builder's chest" keepsake (20 planks, 10 stone). The bonus arrived.

Village on island 250614263 at 2911 s: 22 settlers on two islands. Buildings: HQ, Woodcutter's Hut, Forester's Hut, Quarry, Fisher's Hut, Sawmill and Well on the home island. Harbour, Woodcutter's Hut, Sawmill, House, Fisher's Hut, Stone Mine and Storehouse on the second island. Goods: 140 planks, 31 stone, 38 fish, 161 water, 1 iron, 10 tools, no logs. Current goal: "Bake 20 loaves of bread", 0 / 20.

Things that held up as they should:
- Demolishing a site under construction gave back its full cost.
- Demolishing a working Sawmill returned its logs, fish and tool.
- Building and removing the same hut six times in a row lost no planks and left no settler stranded.
- The HQ and the landing harbour refused to be demolished, with a clear message.
- The trade ship's stock and departure time survived a save and load. Selling iron straight back lost half its value, so the trade has no loop to exploit.
- Loading a save without `?seed=` resumed the newest autosave, as expected.

## How to see each finding again

All steps use the `window.playtest` commands with the game paused (`playtest.speed('pause')`). Seed 250614263 unless noted.

1. Build a woodcutter at 49,47 and a forester at 50,50. Advance 100 s. Press Save. Advance 60 s. Open Saved games and load the save. `playtest.state()` shows every hut "Waiting for a worker".
2. Do finding 1, then run `playtest.snapshot()`. The timeline lists times later than the save, then the save time again.
3. Switch to Polish. Run `playtest.build('woodcutter', 46, 41)`. The returned message is English. The toast on screen is Polish.
4. Switch to Polish. Build a Fisher's Hut at 47,46 and advance 120 s. Open it: "Gotowe: 1 Ryby". A Sawmill shows "Wytworzono 5 Deski".
5. Switch to Polish and open any working hut. The panel shows "⬆️ Ulepszenie" and "⬆️ Ulepsz".
6. Build a Monument once you have 10 planks and 16 stone. When it is finished, open it: "Storing goods".
7. Demolish the finished Monument. Stored planks and stone do not change.
8. Switch to Polish. Open Saved games. Autosaves show "Autosave".
9. Load or make a manual save. Open Saved games and press "Save as new". The list still has one manual save, with the new played time.
10. Build a Sawmill and advance until it saws. Press `stop`. Advance 10 s. Its logs go up.
11. Play into winter (about 1071 s on this seed) with a Fisher's Hut. Advance to just after spring begins (about 1116 s). The hut still says "Fishing, slowly in the cold".
12. Get 14 tools, build a Harbour at 48,44 and press `sail`. About 5 s later the tools warning appears.
13. Sail as in finding 12. After landing, open the new harbour (#329 at 42,30). It holds 16 logs.
14. After landing, demolish the home Harbour. On the second island build a Sawmill at 41,29 and a Storehouse at 44,28. The Storehouse stops at 80% for want of 1 stone.
15. After landing, build a House at 40,28. It waits for food until you build a Fisher's Hut on the second island.
16. Do finding 15 and advance 600 s. `playtest.state()` keeps saying "for 181 s".
17. Open `/?seed=250614263`, play for a while, then reload the page. A fresh island starts.
18. Do finding 15, so settlers live on both islands. Save. The save card shows only the main island's settlers.
19. Open Saved games and leave it open. Press the 💾 Save button on the top bar. The list does not show the new save.
20. Seed 958901770, or any island: build a Woodcutter's Hut and a Sawmill. Advance in 2 s steps until the snapshot shows the woodcutter carrying a log. Demolish the hut. Advance 15 s and count the logs in the HQ, the Sawmill and the new planks. One log is missing.
21. Load a game that has a chronicle, from `/` without a seed. Leave the chronicle open. Press New, pick a keepsake, then switch language. The old island's chronicle is still on the page, in the old language.
22. On a fresh island with no goal reached, press New and accept the confirm dialog. A new island starts at once, with no "Stay on this island" button.
