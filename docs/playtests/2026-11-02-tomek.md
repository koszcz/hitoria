# Playtest: Tomek on island 286117546

- Commit: 7f53284 Start building Fish that run out
- Played: 9 minutes, to game time 7721 s (day 17)
- Goal for this run: use as many different buildings and features as possible

## What the tester achieved

Tomek placed all 22 building types. 21 of them were finished. The Smelter was placed twice, but neither one was ever finished. Tomek also used the trade ship, sailed to both islands, held a wedding feast, and ordered a religious feast.

Goals completed, in order:

1. Make 50 planks: 1155 s
2. Bake 20 bread: 3975 s
3. Grow to 30 settlers: 4486 s
4. Store 30 fish, meat or bread: 4991 s
5. Build a monument: 4991 s. The Monument itself was finished at 2885 s.

Goal not completed: "Get through a winter with no one hungry". It failed in the winter of day 15 (see finding 19).

Features used, with the game time Tomek first used each:

- Lookout Tower: 20 s
- Harbour: 20 s
- Trade ship, 4 planks for 1 iron: 130 s
- Geologist and rich spot: 475 s
- Sailing to the other island: 710 s
- Stop work: 1550 s
- Feast Square and wedding feast: ordered at 4001 s, held at 4546 s, 2 newlyweds at 4776 s
- Sailing to the far island: 5071 s
- Religious feast: ordered at 6611 s. It was still waiting for food when the run ended.

Features Tomek could not use:

- Upgrade: always greyed out (finding 12)
- Smelter: never finished, so no iron was made on any island

Village at the end:

- Population 32, with 1 idle in the HQ.
- Home island: HQ, 3 Houses, 2 Woodcutters, Forester, Sawmill, 2 Quarries, Stone Mine, 3 Fishers, 2 Hunters, 2 Farms, Well, Mill, Bakery, Toolmaker, Storehouse, Feast Square, Monument, 2 Lookout Towers, Harbour and Geologist.
- Other island: Harbour, Coal Mine and Iron Mine, both starving. The Smelter and Toolmaker sites there were stuck at 60% and 50%.
- Far island: Harbour, Fisher, Woodcutter, Sawmill, Forester (stopped), Coal Mine, Iron Mine (starving) and a Smelter site at 0%.
- Goods: plank 252, stone 921, fish 47, grain 11, flour 6, bread 35, water 369, coal 26, meat 12, iron 4, tool 6.

## How to see each finding again

For every finding, open `?seed=286117546`, then run `playtest.speed('pause')`.

1. Run `playtest.card('lookout')`.
2. Run `playtest.build('quarry',47,49)`, then `playtest.advance(120)`. Open building #6.
3. Run `playtest.map(48,48,12)`, then compare it with `playtest.map(42,30,9)`.
4. Run `playtest.build('harbour',49,43)` and advance 60 s. Open the harbour. Press give plank and get iron, then run `playtest.press('trade')`.
5. Do the steps for finding 2. Advance to 1010 s and watch stone stay at 11.
6. Run `playtest.build('woodcutter',50,46)` with no Forester. Advance to 1010 s. Open building #2.
7. Build a harbour at 49,43, a Woodcutter, a Sawmill and a Quarry. Advance until stone is 8 or more and 5 settlers are idle. Press sail. Open the new harbour at 42,30 and read its stock.
8. After step 7, run `build('coalmine',42,27)` and `build('ironmine',46,26)`. Advance 240 s.
9. After step 7, run `playtest.menu()` and compare it with the stock of the harbour at 42,30.
10. Build a Woodcutter at 50,46 and a second one at 46,49. Advance about 940 s after the second one is built.
11. Trade 4 planks for iron on one ship. On the next ship, open the harbour and press give plank once.
12. Buy 4 iron from the trade ship with no Smelter running. Open any hut and read the upgrade section.
13. Follow the build order in "What the tester achieved" up to 1250 s. Then place a Bakery, a Fisher, a Toolmaker, a Storehouse and a Feast Square.
14. Do step 7 with 6 tools. Use up the home tools on new huts. Then compare `playtest.state()` with the home harbour's give list.
15. After step 14, buy 2 tools while a Bakery, a Fisher and a Toolmaker all wait for one.
16. Sail with about 11 tools in stock. Run `playtest.messages()`.
17. Build a Monument and open it when it is finished.
18. Build a Feast Square and order a feast while few settlers are idle. Buy meat from the trade ship.
19. Do step 8. Let a winter pass with food at home.
20. Sail to the far island and build 5 huts there. Advance 240 s.
