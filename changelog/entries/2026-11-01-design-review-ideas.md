A gameplay review looked at what keeps the game calm and what brings players back. Its four small fixes are now one idea in Next: a fairer winter goal, a reward for varied meals, a chronicle of your island's milestones, and better early goals. Its four larger ideas wait in the inbox for triage: village wishes after the last goal, building upgrades, a trade ship with changing wants, and more voyages.
