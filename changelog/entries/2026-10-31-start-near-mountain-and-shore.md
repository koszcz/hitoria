Every new island now has a mountain and a bay inside your starting land. The bay runs out to the
open sea. You can build a stone mine and a fisher from the start, without a lookout tower first.

The land right around your headquarters is always open, so mountains no longer box it in.
The same island number now gives a different map than before. Your saved games stay the same.
