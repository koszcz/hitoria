The island is now a real 3D scene. The camera keeps the same angle, so moving the map, zooming and clicking work as before.

- Land has hills, rock on steep slopes and snow on peaks.
- Water ripples. Land outside your border is darker.
- Buildings are timber-framed houses with roofs, chimneys and lit windows.
- Settlers walk, swing their tools and carry goods.
- Roads are dirt tracks on the ground.
