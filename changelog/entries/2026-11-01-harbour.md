You can now build a Harbour. It sits under "Town" in the build menu, and costs 4 planks and 3 stone.

Build it on the shore, up to 4 tiles from open water. Open water means a patch wide enough for a
ship to turn, so a narrow stream does not count. When it is built, a wooden pier runs from the
harbour out to its dock.

Ships do not come yet. Until they do, the harbour stores goods, just like a storehouse.
