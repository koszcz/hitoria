---
title: Village wishes
date: '2026-11-01 23:59'
---
Once you reach the last goal, your village starts to make wishes. The goal panel shows one wish
at a time, such as "Store 40 fish, meat or bread" or "Welcome 2 trade ships". It shows how close
you are, and the season to meet it by, such as "before the first snow".

When you meet a wish, it counts as granted, and the village makes a new wish. If the season
comes first, the wish just passes, with no penalty, and a new one comes. The panel shows how many
wishes you have granted, and your save keeps the wish you are on.
