The Feast Square can now hold a religious feast as well as a wedding feast. Select a finished
square and press **Order a religious feast**.

It works like a wedding feast. Carriers bring 10 fish, 10 bread and 10 meat, and at the next dusk
every worker feasts at the square until midnight. Their huts say "At the religious feast".

At dawn, no new settlers arrive. Instead, the village is blessed for the whole day:

- Every worker works 25% faster. Walking takes as long as before.
- Every builder builds 25% faster.
- The blessing ends at midnight.

While the blessing lasts, each hut's panel and the square's panel say so. The two feasts share
one limit: you can hold one feast each season, of either kind. Your save keeps a planned feast and
the blessing.
