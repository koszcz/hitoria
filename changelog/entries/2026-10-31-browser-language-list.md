---
title: Start in a language from your browser's list
date: '2026-10-31 20:30'
---
The first time you open Hitoria, it now looks at every language set in your browser, in your
order. It starts in the first one it speaks. For example, if your browser lists German, then
Polish, the game starts in Polish. Before, it looked only at your first language. If you pick a
language yourself, the game still remembers it.
