---
title: The trade ship keeps daytime hours
date: '2026-11-01 19:15'
---
The trade ship now comes once a day. It sails in each morning and stays tied up at your harbour
until late afternoon. Then it sails away before sunset.

Before, the ship came every few minutes, so a visit could fall at night, when the clock runs
fast. The ship then left before you had time to trade. Now you have the whole day to trade with it.

If your harbour is finished after midday, the first ship comes the next morning. The harbour
panel says how long you have to wait.
