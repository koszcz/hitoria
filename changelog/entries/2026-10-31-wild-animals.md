Deer, hares and foxes now live on the island. Deer graze in small herds on open meadows,
hares sit alone in the grass, and foxes keep near the edge of the woods.

Each animal wanders near its own spot and stops to graze now and then. When a settler comes
close, it runs a few steps away. Animals never get in your settlers' way.
