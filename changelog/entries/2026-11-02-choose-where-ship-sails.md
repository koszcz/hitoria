---
title: Choose where the ship sails
date: '2026-11-02 23:50'
---
You now pick where your settlers sail. The harbour's panel shows one Sail button for each island
nobody has settled yet: "Sail to the other island" and "Sail to the far island". The ship lands
on the island you pick, so you can go straight to the far island and its rich coal and iron.

Once settlers live on one island, its button goes away, and the next ship can sail only to the
island that is left. Older saves, which have no far island, keep their single Set sail button.
