The sea now feels alive. White foam runs along every beach, and small waves roll in toward the
sand. Now and then a fish leaps out of the water and lands with a splash ring. Flocks of ducks
paddle slow loops near the shore, and sometimes dip their heads under to feed.

The ducks fly south for the winter and come back in spring. The fish and ducks are only there to
look at. They don't change the game.
