---
title: Work sounds
date: '2026-11-01 23:30'
---
You now hear your settlers work. Woodcutters chop, the sawmill saws, and builders, stonecutters
and the toolmaker hammer. You hear only the work near the middle of the screen, and nearer work
is louder. The sounds fade as you zoom out, and stop while the game is paused.

The game makes these sounds in code, like the wind and waves, so there are no sound files.
Press M to mute all sound, work sounds included.
