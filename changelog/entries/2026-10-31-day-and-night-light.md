The island now has days and nights. A full day lasts 8 minutes of game time, and a new game
starts in the morning.

The light turns warm orange at dawn and dusk, and cool blue at night. The night is dim, but you
can still see your village and keep playing. The sun moves across the sky during the day, so
shadows turn and grow long in the evening.

The clock runs on game time. It stops when you pause, runs faster at higher game speeds, and a
saved game keeps its time of day.
