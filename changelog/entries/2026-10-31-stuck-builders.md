---
title: Builders no longer get stuck at sites without a road
date: '2026-10-31 18:30'
---
A builder now waits in the HQ until a building site has a road to your HQ. If a site loses its road, its builder goes back to the HQ to help elsewhere. Before, a builder could wait at a site without a road for good, and the HQ could run out of settlers to build anything else.

Houses now get a builder before other sites, because only houses bring in new settlers.

The debug panel now warns you when buildings wait for a settler and nobody is idle in the HQ.
