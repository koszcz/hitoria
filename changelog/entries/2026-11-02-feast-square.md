You can now build a Feast Square, with a long table, a maypole, bunting and two lanterns. It
costs 4 planks and 4 stone, and you find it under Town in the build menu.

Select a finished square and press **Order a wedding feast**. Then:

1. Carriers bring 10 fish, 10 bread and 10 meat to the square. The panel shows how much has
   arrived. The square gets food after every other building, so a feast never starves your workers.
2. At the next dusk, the food is served. Every worker and builder walks to the square instead of
   going to bed, and their huts say "At the wedding feast". Carriers keep working.
3. At midnight the guests go to bed.
4. At dawn, 2 new settlers join your village. They need no house.

You can order one feast each season, and only one feast can be planned at a time. Your save keeps
a planned feast. The first wedding feast also gets a line in the island chronicle.
