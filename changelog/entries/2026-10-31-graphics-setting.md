The game draws buildings and settlers with far less work, so it runs smoother, most of all on
phones and tablets. A busy village now takes about a fifth of the drawing work it did before.

There is a new **Graphics** button in the menu. Click it to pick Low, Medium or High, or Auto.
Auto is the default. It starts on a level that suits your device, and if the game runs slowly it
lowers the graphics while you play and tells you. Low turns off shadows and draws fewer pixels.
Press F3 to see how much the game draws each frame.
