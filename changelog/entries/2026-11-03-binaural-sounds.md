---
title: Work sounds come from where they happen
date: '2026-11-03 19:32'
---
Chopping, sawing and hammering now come from where the work happens. Work to the right of you on
the screen plays in your right ear, and work to the left plays in your left ear. Work near you is
louder.

On a computer, you hear from the mouse pointer. Move it over a busy corner of the village to hear
that work up close. On a phone, or when the pointer leaves the window, you hear from the middle of
the screen.
