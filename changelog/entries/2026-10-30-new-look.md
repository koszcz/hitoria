The whole game has a new look, picked in five rounds of side-by-side choices.

- Colours are earthy and muted, like the classic Settlers games. The light is soft and the ground has a painted, speckled texture.
- Every building has its own shape. Houses have a stone ground floor, a timber frame above, and tiled, slate or thatched roofs. The HQ is a stone keep with a round tower.
- Smoke rises from chimneys while a building works. The sawmill's blade turns.
- Building sites have scaffolding, a ladder and a frame that rises as the work goes on.
- Settlers are bigger, with big heads and a hat and tool for their trade. Carriers hold loads on their heads.
- Trees, rocks, goods and flags are redrawn. The top bar shows goods as small pictures.
