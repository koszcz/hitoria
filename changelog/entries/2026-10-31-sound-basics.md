The island now has sound. You hear soft wind, and it grows louder as gusts pass. Near the shore you
hear waves roll in. Both sounds get quieter when you zoom out.

To mute the sound, click **Sound** in the menu or press M. The game remembers your choice.
The sound starts after your first click or key press, because browsers block sound until then.
