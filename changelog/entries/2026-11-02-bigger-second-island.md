On new maps, the second island is about 20 tiles across instead of 13. It has about twice the
land, with wider meadows and more forest. Its mountain ridge now runs across the middle of the
island and is about three times as long, so it holds about twice as much coal and iron ore. You
can walk around both ends of the ridge to the meadows behind it. The island sits a little farther
out, so the strait between it and your home island stays as wide as before. Games you saved
before this change keep their smaller island.
