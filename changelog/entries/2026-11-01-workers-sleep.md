---
title: Workers sleep at night
date: '2026-11-01 23:50'
---
Your workers now sleep at night. When dusk ends, each worker and builder finishes the work in
hand and walks to the nearest bed: the HQ, a finished house or the harbour on the second island.
Their hut or building site says "Asleep until dawn", and work stops until morning.

At dawn they walk back to their huts and sites and go on working. Carriers and pack donkeys work
all night, so goods still move to where they are needed.
