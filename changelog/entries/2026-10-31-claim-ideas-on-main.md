---
title: Claim ideas on main
date: '2026-10-31 18:00'
kind: dev
---
Parallel sessions no longer build the same idea. `/build` and `/triage` now bring in the latest backlog from `main` first. `/build` then claims its idea by pushing the move to **Building now** straight to `main`, with the branch name on the line. **Building now** can hold one idea for each branch.
