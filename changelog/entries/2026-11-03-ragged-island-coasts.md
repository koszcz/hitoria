On new maps, the second island and the far island are no longer round.

- Each island has its own outline, with capes that reach out to sea and deep bays that cut into
  the land.
- About half of the islands also have a long arm of land, with sea on both sides.
- Every new map gives the two islands a different shape. Both islands still have about the same
  amount of land, and you can walk to every part of each one.

Saved games keep the map they were made on.
