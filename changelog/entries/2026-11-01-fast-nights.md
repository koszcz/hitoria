Nights now pass quickly. Once every worker and builder is in bed, the clock runs 8 times as fast,
or 16 times if you already play at 2× or 4×. At dawn it drops back to the speed you chose.
While a night runs fast, the moon button next to the speed buttons turns blue and shows the speed.

Fast nights are on when you start. Click the moon button to turn them off or on again.
The game remembers your choice.
