On new maps, the far island is about 26 tiles across instead of 13, so it is now the largest of
the three islands. It has more than twice the land, with wider meadows and more forest. Its
mountain ridge runs across the island and is about four times as long, so it holds about twice
as much coal and iron ore. The ridge sits a little towards the shore where your ship lands, so
coal and iron mines fit near the landing. You can walk around both ends of the ridge to the
meadows behind it. The island sits farther out, so the sea around it stays as wide as before.

On a pine island, the pines now leave a wider clear strip at the foot of the ridge, so coal and
iron mines always have room there. Games you saved before this change keep their smaller far
island.
