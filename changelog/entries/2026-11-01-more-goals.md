title: More goals
date: '2026-11-01 18:00'
---
After the winter goal, a second set of goals leads you to the other island and its riches:

1. Settle the other island. Your settlers reach it when their ship lands and they build their harbour.
2. Mine 20 coal.
3. Mine 20 iron ore.
4. Make 10 iron at a smelter.

Coal and iron ore lie only in the mountains of the other island, so you settle it first.
Only coal, iron ore and iron your settlers make count, not what you have in store.
