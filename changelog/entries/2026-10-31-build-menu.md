---
title: Build menu
date: '2026-10-31 19:00'
---
The bar at the bottom of the screen is now short: **🔨 Build**, **🛤️ Road** and **✖️ Remove**.

- Press **🔨 Build**, or the B key, to open the build menu. It sorts buildings into Wood, Stone,
  Food and Town.
- With a mouse, point at a building to read about it, then click it to pick it.
- On a phone, tap a building to read about it. Tap it again, or tap the button below, to pick it.
- A cost shows in orange when you don't have enough goods for it yet.
- While you place a building, the Build button shows which one you picked.
- The number keys 1 to 9 still pick a building straight away.
