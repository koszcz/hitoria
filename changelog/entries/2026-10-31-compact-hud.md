The top bar now takes one row, so you can see more of your island.

- Goods show as a picture and a number. Point at a good to see its name.
- Goods you have none of are dimmed.
- Save, saves, new island, report, graphics, sound and language are in the ☰ menu at the top
  right. Ctrl+S still saves, and M still mutes the sound.
