A small clock now sits under the speed buttons at the top right. On a phone it sits in the top bar.
Its dial is light on top and dark below. A hand carries the sun across the light half by day and
the moon across the dark half by night, so you can see how long until dusk or dawn.

Next to the dial you see the season, a thin bar that fills as the season goes on, and the day
count. The count starts at day 1 and goes up at midnight.
