You can now build a Smelter. It sits with the mines, under "Stone and ore" in the build menu.

The smelter melts 1 iron ore and 1 coal into 1 bar of iron every 6 seconds. Carriers bring it
both goods, and the furnace beside it glows and smokes while it works. If one of the two goods
is missing, its panel says which one it is waiting for.

Iron now shows in the goods bar. Nothing uses iron yet, so it waits in your stores.
