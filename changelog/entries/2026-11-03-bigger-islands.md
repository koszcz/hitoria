On new maps, the second island and the far island are both about 32 tiles across, the same size.
Each has about half the land of your home island, so a colony has room to grow.

- Each island has a mountain ridge about twice as long as before, with more coal and iron ore.
- The ridge sits on the side that faces your home island, so coal and iron mines still fit in the
  land around your landing harbour.
- Each island has more forest or meadow than before, on the side away from home.
- The map is now 128 by 128 tiles, with open sea around all three islands.

Saved games keep the map they were made on.
