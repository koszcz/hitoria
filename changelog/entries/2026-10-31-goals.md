Your island now has five peaceful goals to work through, one after another:

1. Make 50 planks.
2. Bake 20 bread.
3. Grow to 30 settlers.
4. Build 3 houses.
5. Build a monument.

A panel at the top left shows the goal you are on and how close you are. When you reach a goal,
a message tells you, and the next goal starts. Only goods your settlers make count, not the goods
you start with.

The new Monument is a tall stone obelisk with a gold tip. It costs 10 planks and 16 stone, and it
does no work. Your saved games keep your goals.
