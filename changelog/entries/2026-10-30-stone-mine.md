A stone mine sits next to a mountain and never runs out. Its miner eats one fish or one bread for every 2 stone. Four new buildings make that food:

- a fisher's hut near water
- a farm that sows and harvests grain
- a mill that grinds grain into flour
- a bakery that bakes bread

Trees and crops now grow at the right speed. They used to grow about 10% too slowly.
