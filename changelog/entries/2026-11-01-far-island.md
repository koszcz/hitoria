New maps now have a third island, the far island. It lies farther out than the other island,
to one side of the bay, with open sea around the edge of the map.

- **Sail twice.** Once your first settlers have landed and the ship is back, the harbour's
  panel offers a second voyage. It takes 5 more settlers, 12 planks, 8 stone and 6 tools to
  the island nobody has settled yet.
- **Rich ore.** The far island's mountain holds coal and iron ore, and all of it is rich, so
  every mine there digs twice as fast.

Saved games keep their land. Islands from before this change have no far island, so their
settlers still sail only once.
