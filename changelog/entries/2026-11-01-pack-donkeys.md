When many goods wait to move, pack donkeys now join your carriers. A donkey carries two goods at
once, one in each basket on its back, and walks the same worn paths as your settlers.

- Donkeys come out when at least 8 goods wait for a carrier, such as the planks and stone for a
  big building site.
- You get one donkey for every 4 buildings, counting the HQ.
- Donkeys are not settlers. They live in a stall at the HQ, so they never take a worker from you.
- When the work is done, the donkeys walk back to the HQ.
- The first donkey of a game tells you it has joined.
