Every new worker in a hut now needs a tool, so you can't grow for free.

- The HQ starts with 20 tools. The goods bar shows how many you have left.
- When a hut gets its worker, the worker takes 1 tool from the nearest store and brings it to
  the hut. The tool stays there, so the hut never needs another one.
- If no store has a tool, the hut's panel says "Waiting for a tool".
- Scouts in lookout towers need no tool. Builders and carriers need none either.
- If you demolish a hut, its tool goes back to your stores.
- The ship that settles the other island now also takes 6 tools.

You can now build a Toolmaker's Workshop. It turns 1 iron and 1 plank into 1 tool every
8 seconds. You find it with the mines, under "Stone and ore" in the build menu.

Saves from before this change keep working. Huts that already have a worker keep it, and your
HQ gets 20 tools.
