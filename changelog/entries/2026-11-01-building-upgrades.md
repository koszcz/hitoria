You can now upgrade your buildings with iron and tools. Select a finished hut or house, and its
panel shows an Upgrade button. An upgrade costs 2 iron and 1 tool from your stores, and each
building upgrades once.

- In an upgraded hut, mine or workshop, the worker takes half as long over each piece of work.
  Walking takes as long as before, and the worker eats as much for each good they make.
- An upgraded house has room for 2 more settlers, so it adds 6 in all instead of 4.

Until your settlers sail to the other island, the game keeps back the tools the ship needs. So
iron and tools now have a use after the last goal.
