---
title: Play on your phone
date: '2026-10-31 18:30'
---
You can now play Hitoria on a phone or tablet.

- Drag with one finger to move around the island. Pinch with two fingers to zoom.
- To build, pick a building, tap a spot to see where it goes, then tap the same spot again.
  Roads work the same way.
- Tap **✋ Done** to put a building or tool away.
- On a small screen, the Save, Saves, New, Report and language buttons sit behind the **☰** menu.
  Goods show as a picture and a number, so more of the island fits on the screen.
- The game also fits a phone turned on its side.
