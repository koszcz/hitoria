On a new map, the second island and the far island each get their own theme, so they look and
play differently. Which island gets which theme is random.

- **Pine highlands**: dense, dark pine forest, with about twice the trees, and bare rock cliffs
  along the shore that faces the open sea. Woodcutters have plenty to fell here.
- **Wild meadows**: wide meadows full of flowers and only a small grove of trees. More deer and
  hares live here, so it is a good place for hunters.

Both islands keep their mountain with coal and iron ore. Saved games keep the land they had.
