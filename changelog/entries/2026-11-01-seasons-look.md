The island now goes through spring, summer, autumn and winter. A full year lasts 20 minutes of
game time, and a new game starts in spring.

- **Spring**: the grass is fresh and bright, and some trees are in pink blossom.
- **Summer**: the grass and trees are a deep green.
- **Autumn**: the grass dries to gold. Leafy trees turn yellow, orange and red, each at its own pace.
- **Winter**: leafy trees lose their leaves. Snow falls, and it covers the ground, rocks and firs.
  Small plants and flowers hide under the snow until it melts in spring.

The seasons only change the look. Your settlers work the same all year. The year runs on game
time, so it stops when you pause, and a saved game keeps its season.
