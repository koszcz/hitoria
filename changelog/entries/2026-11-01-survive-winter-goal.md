---
title: Survive winter goal
date: '2026-11-01 12:00'
---
After you build the monument, a new last goal asks you to get through a winter with no one
hungry.

- The goal counts one whole winter, from the first snow to spring. If a winter has already
  begun when you reach the goal, wait for the next one.
- A settler goes hungry when a house or mine waits more than 20 seconds for food in winter.
  You then see "🥶 A settler went hungry this winter", and you can try again next winter.
- Under the goal, a line tells you how it is going: store food before winter, no one hungry
  yet, or try again next winter.
- Full houses eat nothing, so they never go hungry.
