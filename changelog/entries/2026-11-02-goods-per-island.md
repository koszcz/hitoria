---
title: Goods per island
date: '2026-11-02 23:30'
---
The goods bar and the build menu now show the goods of the island you are looking at, not every
island added together. Settlers on one island cannot use goods that sit on another, so you can now
see what a new colony really has before you place a building there.

Once a second island has a store, the goods bar names the island you are looking at, such as
"Second island", and so does the build menu's title. Pan the camera to another island to see its
goods. Over open sea or an island with no store yet, you see the home island. Goals and wishes
still count the goods on every island.
