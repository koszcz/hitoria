Islands have a third theme: the hidden cove. On a new map, the second island and the far island
each get one of three themes, pine highlands, wild meadows or the hidden cove, and never the same
one.

- **Hidden cove**: a sheltered lagoon cuts deep into the island from the open sea, through a narrow
  mouth. Sand and thick reeds ring its shore.
- A fisher who fishes from the lagoon's shore brings home 2 fish from each catch instead of 1. A
  fisher's hut with the lagoon in reach goes there first, and its panel says "Fishing in the cove".
- More ducks paddle and more fish jump in the lagoon than anywhere else.

Saved games keep the land they had.
