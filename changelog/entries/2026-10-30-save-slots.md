You can now keep several saved games. The new Saves window lets you load, save as new, overwrite or delete saves. You can also choose how often the game autosaves: every minute, every 5 minutes, or never.

Autosaves take turns over 3 slots, so one bad moment can't wipe out your only copy. Starting a new island autosaves the old one first.
