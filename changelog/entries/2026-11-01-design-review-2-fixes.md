---
title: Fairer wishes, a fuller chronicle and smaller fixes
date: '2026-11-01 23:59'
---
Village wishes are fairer. The trade wish now asks for 1 ship. A store wish asks for more than
you hold when it starts, so it is never granted for free. New wishes ask you to make 10 iron,
upgrade 2 buildings, sell the trader 8 of the goods it wants, and land on the far island. The
village makes a wish only when you can grant it.

The island chronicle records more milestones: the first wish granted and every 5th after it,
the first upgrade, each landing on an island, each winter with no one hungry, and 50 and 100
settlers.

Other fixes:

- A worker who rests or sleeps is no longer marked hungry. The winter goal does not check for
  hunger at night.
- You can upgrade a building only after you have made iron yourself.
- Foresters no longer plant trees on worn paths.
- The HQ keeps 6 tools back until the ship has sailed to every island, not only the first.
