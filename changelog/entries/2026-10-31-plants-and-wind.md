The island has more to look at than trees and boulders.

- Tall grass and patches of flowers grow in the meadows. Bushes with red berries grow at the edge of the woods.
- The forest floor has ferns, mushrooms and fallen logs. You see them once the trees around them are felled.
- Reeds grow on the shore, and pebbles lie on sand and mountains.
- Some leafy trees are white birches, and a few are in pink blossom.
- Wind gusts roll across the island and bend the trees, grass, reeds and grain.

Plants are only there to look at. They never block a building, road or field. They hide under anything you put on their tile, and come back when it's gone.
