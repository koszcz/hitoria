Grain now grows only in the warm months, so you need to store food before winter comes.

- In spring, summer and autumn, farms sow and harvest grain as before.
- In winter, the fields rest. Farmers still bring in grain that ripened before the frost, but
  they sow nothing. The farm's panel says "Fields rest in winter".
- Grain that was sown but not ripe waits under the snow, and grows again in spring.

Mills, bakeries, houses and mines keep working through the winter, as long as they have grain
and food in store.
