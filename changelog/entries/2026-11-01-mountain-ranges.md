Mountains now form ranges. High ground folds into long, bending ridges that run side by side,
with grassy valleys between them, instead of round lumps. The mountain by your HQ is now a short
ridge that runs away from the HQ, and the mountain on the other island is a short ridge too.

Ridges fade out near your HQ, so the land you start on stays gentle, and no ridge is steep
enough to look like a cliff. Trees and boulders no longer grow on the bank of the bay near your
HQ, so you always have room for a harbour there.

New games get the new land. Saved games keep the land they have.
