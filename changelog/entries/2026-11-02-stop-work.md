---
title: Stop work at a hut
date: '2026-11-02 12:00'
---
Every finished hut with a worker now has a **Stop work** button in its panel. Use it when you run
short of settlers, such as to stop a stone mine while you have plenty of stone.

When you press it:

- The worker walks home and takes the next hut that needs a worker.
- Nobody is sent to the stopped hut, day or night, until you press **Resume work**.
- The hut keeps its tool, so the next worker does not need a new one.
- A good in the worker's hands stays at the hut. A workshop that was in the middle of a job
  gets its inputs back.
- Carriers bring the stopped hut no food, because nobody is there to eat it.

The hut says "Work stopped", and your save keeps the choice.
