When you place a building, it now gets a road right away. The road takes the shortest way to the nearest flag or road that already leads to your HQ. If it meets a road, it joins that road with a new flag instead of running beside it.

While you choose a spot, a dashed line shows the road the building will get. You can still draw extra roads with the road tool.
