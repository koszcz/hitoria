When you start a new island, a message tells you that your workers eat fish, meat or bread, and
that they work at half speed without food. It suggests you build a Fisher's Hut near the shore
early. The message stays on screen longer than other messages, so you have time to read it.

The Fisher's Hut, Hunter's Lodge and Bakery cards now list every hut whose worker eats that food.
