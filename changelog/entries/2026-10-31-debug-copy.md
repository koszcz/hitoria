---
title: Copy button in the debug panel
date: '2026-10-31 18:00'
kind: dev
---
The debug panel (F3) now has a "Copy for Claude" button. It copies a full snapshot of the
settlement: every building with its status, worker, goods and what is in range, every settler
out of the HQ, goods on flags, roads, and recent events. The panel also shows how many settlers
are idle in the HQ.
