---
title: Rates in the info panel
date: '2026-11-02 15:19'
---
A building's panel now shows how fast it works. Under **In the last minute**, it lists the goods
the building made and the goods it used up, such as "Made 12 Water" and "Used 1 Fish".

- Goods a workshop turns into something else count as used.
- Food that a worker eats, and food that a house turns into a new settler, counts as used too.
- A hut that made and used nothing in the last minute says so. This helps you spot a hut that
  is stuck or waiting.
- Buildings that never make or use goods, such as a lookout tower, show no rates.

The game counts again from zero after you load a save.
