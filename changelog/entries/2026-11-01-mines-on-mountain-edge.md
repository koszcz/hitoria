You can now build a mine on the edge of a mountain, not only on the grass beside it. The tile in
front of the mine's door must still be open grass or sand.

A mine digs into the mountain up to 2 tiles away. From the edge, it reaches rich spots that sit
deeper in the mountain, where no mine on the grass could reach them. Before, some gold signs
marked rich spots you could never use.

The geologist's sign on a tile is hidden once a mine stands on it.
