A second gameplay review checked how the first review's fixes landed and how the new late-game systems fit together. Its seven small fixes are now one idea at the top of Next: hunger that clears while workers rest or sleep, wishes that can all be granted, wishes that use upgrades, iron and the far island, more chronicle milestones, upgrades that need your own iron, foresters that keep off worn paths, and tools kept back for every voyage. Two larger ideas wait in the inbox: a keepsake for each granted wish, and choosing where the ship sails. The religious feast moved to Later to make room in Next.
