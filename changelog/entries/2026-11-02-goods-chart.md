You can now see how your goods change over time. Click the goods bar at the top, or press
**G**, to open the goods chart.

- The chart draws one line for each good you pick. It starts with logs, planks, stone and fish.
- Click a good above the chart to show or hide its line.
- The chart covers the last hour of play, and it keeps drawing while the game runs.
- Press G, Escape or **Back to the game** to close it.

The chart starts again from the moment you load a save.
