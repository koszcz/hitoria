Some mountain tiles are now rich spots. A mine next to a rich spot digs twice as fast, so it
pays to choose where you put your mines.

You can now build a Geologist's Hut near a mountain. You find it with the mines, under "Stone and
ore" in the build menu. Its geologist walks to the mountain tiles within 6 tiles of the hut and
puts up a sign on each one:

- A gold sign with a gold knob on top marks a rich spot.
- A pale sign marks plain rock.
- The lumps on the sign show what lies below: a grey stone for stone, or a black lump of coal and
  a red lump of iron ore.

When the geologist finds a new patch of rich ground, a message tells you. When you place a mine
next to a rich spot, a message tells you too, and the mine's panel says it digs twice as fast.
Rich spots work even where no geologist has looked yet.

Every island has a rich spot on the mountain by the HQ and on the ore mountain of the other
island. Saves from before this change keep working, and get rich spots too.
