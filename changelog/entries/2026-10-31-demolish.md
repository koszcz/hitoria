You can now demolish a building you placed in the wrong spot. Pick the Remove tool (X) and click the building.

- All goods in the building go back to your HQ, including building materials that already arrived.
- Its worker or builder walks back to the HQ.
- The building's flag and roads stay, so you can build something else there.
- Demolishing a lookout tower gives back the land it added.
- You can't demolish your HQ.
