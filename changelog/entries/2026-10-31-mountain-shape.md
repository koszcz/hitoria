Mountains now rise smoothly out of the land around them, through gentle foothills, to a peak.
There is no cliff wall at a mountain's edge, and no random spikes on its sides.
Mountains near your HQ, the bay, the grove and the quarry now slope down to them. So they sit
a tile or two further away than before.

New games get the new shape. Saved games keep their land, but their mountains are drawn
more smoothly too.
