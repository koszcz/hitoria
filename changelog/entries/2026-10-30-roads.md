Goods now move only along roads. Each road section has its own carrier, who passes goods from flag to flag.

- Every building has a flag at its door.
- Press `R` to draw a road and `X` to remove one.
- A building with no road to the HQ gets no materials.
- Settlers walk faster on roads.
