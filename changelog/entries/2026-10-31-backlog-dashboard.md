A new page shows every idea in `ideas/backlog.md` at a glance. It has the ideas being built, the next three and the inbox, a fun and effort grid, and the ideas in Later with a filter for ideas that are ready to build. Ideas that wait for another idea are marked in red.

Open it at `/backlog` on the dev server, or run `bun run backlog` to write `backlog.html`. The site build also puts it online next to the changelog.
