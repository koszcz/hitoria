The harbour panel no longer runs off the bottom of the screen, so you can trade again.

- While the trade ship is tied up, the trade part of the panel shows in a second column beside the rest.
- Stores show their goods as pictures and numbers, five to a row, instead of one line each. Hold the mouse over a good to see its name.
