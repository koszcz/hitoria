You can now build a Hunter's Lodge. Its hunter walks out to a deer or hare up to 7 tiles away,
creeps up on it and brings home meat. Foxes are left alone.

Meat is food, like fish and bread. Houses turn it into new settlers, and mines feed it to
their miners.

Caught animals come back. About once a minute, a new one appears where a caught animal used
to live, so a hunter's meadow fills up again.
