A calm tune now plays in the background: soft bells over slow, warm chords. The game writes the
notes in code, so there are no music files to load.

To change how loud the music is, click **Music** in the ☰ menu. Each click moves it one step, from
Off up to 100%. It starts at 50%, and the game remembers your choice. Press M to mute all sound,
music included.
