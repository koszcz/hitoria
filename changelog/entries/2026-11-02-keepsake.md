---
title: Fresh island with a keepsake
date: '2026-11-02 14:38'
---
When you start a new island, you can now bring one keepsake from the island you leave.

You earn each keepsake by reaching a goal:

| Keepsake | Earned by | What the new island starts with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Builder's crate | Make 50 planks | 20 more planks and 10 more stone |
| Old friends | Grow to 30 settlers | 6 more settlers |
| Full larder | Get through a winter with no one hungry | 20 fish and 10 bread |
| Iron chest | Make 10 iron | 5 iron and 8 more tools |

How it works:

1. Press **New** in the menu, and confirm.
2. If you earned any keepsakes, a dialog lists them. Pick one. The game waits while you choose.
3. To keep playing the old island instead, press **Stay on this island** or Escape.

If you earned no keepsake yet, the new island starts at once, as before. The new island's
chronicle notes which keepsake it began with and which island it came from.

Behind the scenes, the changelog now reads the commit of every entry with one git call, so its
test no longer times out.
