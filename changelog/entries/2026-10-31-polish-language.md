You can now play Hitoria in Polish. Click **🇵🇱 Polski** at the top right to switch, and
**🇬🇧 English** to switch back. The game remembers your choice. If your browser is set to Polish,
the game starts in Polish.

Everything you read while playing is translated: the build bar, building cards, building
statuses, messages and the Saves dialog.
