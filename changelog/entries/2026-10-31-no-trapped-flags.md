---
title: No more flags trapped by trees
date: '2026-10-31 16:00'
---
You can no longer place a building where trees wall in its flag. The build cursor now says "No road can reach it" when no road could ever join the building to your HQ. Young saplings count as trees here, because roads can't cross them either. Foresters also stop planting right next to flags, so a flag that has room for a road keeps it.
