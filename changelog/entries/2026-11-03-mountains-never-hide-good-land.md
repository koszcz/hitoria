On new maps, mountains no longer hide grass you could build on.

- On the second island and the far island, the mountain ridge bends along the shore at the top of
  the screen. The sea lies behind it, and the rest of the island lies in front of it, in full view.
- On your home island, the mountain near the HQ always stands above the HQ on the screen, with the
  bay below it. The other mountains rise only in the upper part of the island. The HQ's land still
  has room for a Stone Mine.
- Any ground that a peak still hides from view is now bare rock. Nothing grows there, and you
  can't build on it.
- Your ship now lands on a shore with coal and iron close by, so Coal and Iron Mines fit in the
  land around your new harbour.

Saved games keep the map they were made on.
